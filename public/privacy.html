MiHarina – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

MiHarina is a personal expense-tracking app. This policy explains what data it accesses, what it stores, and how you can have it removed.

What we access

If you connect your Gmail account, MiHarina reads only email messages sent by specific bank and payment senders, in order to extract transaction details: amount, merchant, date and currency. It does not read, use or process any other email.

What we store

We store the parsed expense data (amount, merchant, date, currency, category) in our own database. We do not store the full contents of your emails.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your data with anyone, with one exception: the text of a transaction notification is sent to OpenAI, an LLM provider, solely to categorize the expense. It is not used for advertising, and we do not allow it to be used to build profiles of you.

Google API Services User Data Policy

MiHarina's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the expense-tracking feature you requested; it is not used for advertising and is not transferred to others except as described above or as required by law.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept until you ask us to delete it. You can revoke MiHarina's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To request deletion of your stored data, email toche.cr@gmail.com; we will delete it within 30 days.
